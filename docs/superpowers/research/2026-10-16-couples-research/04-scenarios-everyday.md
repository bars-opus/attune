# 04 — Everyday couple scenarios: 14 archetypes × 8 weeks in Attune

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Method:** scenario simulation by a relationship-science / couples-therapy / product lens, grounded strictly in `01-market-reviews.md` (market), `02-relationship-science.md` (evidence), `03-attune-inventory.md` (what exists and its status), and the binding rules in `ATTUNE_SOUL.md`, `ATTUNE_MASTER_SPEC.md §11`, `ATTUNE_THESIS.md`. Read-only; no app files changed.

**How to read a scenario.** (a) is what the couple would realistically do *today* with the shipped product and where it lands well. (b) is where it fails, frustrates, is missing or risks harm, with inventory statuses cited (LIVE / DEPLOY-PENDING / FLAG-OFF / BUILT-UNREACHABLE / SPEC-ONLY / Absent). (c) is what the evidence says would help, with the 02 grade. (d) is what the market says users love or hate in this exact situation. (e) is concrete changes: tab or screen, copy tone, data and privacy notes. (f) is priority (P0 harm or blocker, P1 high value across archetypes, P2 valuable, P3 nice) and confidence.

**Binding rules every recommendation was checked against** (03 §1): no scorecards or partner-vs-partner counts; no streaks, day counters or "days apart"; proposals not impositions for anything touching the partner; at most one scheduled push per surface per day; discreet device surfaces off by default; AI never interprets the relationship, takes sides or acts as a companion (Decision 28); safety detection keyword-only and the at-risk recipient is the only one notified; no couple report; asymmetric data self-facing only; no diagnostic labels; translator invisible to the recipient; nothing celebratory fires from message content.

**Shared baseline the scenarios assume** (from 03): Planning tab LIVE (categories, 13 blocks, gallery templates Next visit / Moving house / Wedding / Baby, per-entry money with no cross-entry totals, no recurrence engine). Calendar/Together LIVE (events me/partner/us, proposals, "Needs you", big-day care cards for *stressful* days only, 08:00 daily summary as the one scheduled push, five ritual templates, Story memory prompts, "On this day", reminders and family birthdays, busy overlay and free-window finder 14 days ahead). Together AI capture and ideas FLAG-OFF. Widgets, share sheet, Live Activity and calendar mirror DEPLOY-PENDING. Map committed but `db push` pending (≈1 km area, update only on app open, hidden after 8 h, symmetric silent pause, reunion countdown, dual clock, story pins, "I'm at…" chat card, distance widget). Chat LIVE with media, voice, video, view-once, reactions, pins, translator; read receipts always on with **no opt-out**; a conversation-streak counter computed but not rendered. Stories LIVE (realtime refetch not wired). Pulse PARTIAL (weekly cron paused; check-in reminder a `debugPrint` stub). Verdict BUILT-UNREACHABLE. Attachment quiz reachable only via Pulse; love-languages, communication and conflict quizzes BUILT-UNREACHABLE. Love map LIVE (3 prompts/week). 36 Questions LIVE. Games LIVE (Mirror, Sliding Scale with 6 statements, Scenario, This-or-That 50/230, Truth-or-Dare 50/250, Paint Ball). Ask Attune Ideas/Nearby/Understand and AI Sessions LIVE; AI refuses religion, medical, financial, adult venues. Journal DEPLOY-PENDING with no safety check. Safety: 9 placeholder phrases, English only, resources for Ghana/US/UK only, quick exit without PIN, no self-harm protocol. No global quiet hours. **No couple type or couple profile in the schema.**

## 1. Long-distance, 7 time zones apart, end date uncertain

**Ama (Accra, GMT) and Daniel (Vancouver, GMT−7).** Together 14 months, apart for 9. Daniel's visa situation means nobody can say when the distance ends. He visits in week 5 for ten days.

### (a) Week by week

| Wk | What they actually do | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daniel creates a **Next visit** plan from the Planning gallery: Countdown to 14 Nov, Date poll for which weekend to go to Cape Coast, Dual clock, Expenses with two flight lines, "Things to do together" checklist. Ama opts into map presence; Daniel does too. | Delight: the template is exactly their shape. Dual clock in Planning and on the Map distance card ends the "what time is it for you" arithmetic. Countdown is anticipation, not a count of time apart (allowed by the distance-widget rule). |
| 2 | Evening Accra = morning Vancouver. They use the **free-window finder** (busy overlay on, strips) to find two 45-minute overlaps a week. Ama sets a **Weekly call** ritual, Sunday 20:00 Accra. Stories: Ama posts her trotro commute; Daniel his snow. | Delight: finder respects both zones (08:00–22:00 each side). Stories' "seen / not seen only" avoids read-pressure. Friction: Stories realtime refetch isn't wired, so Daniel sees Ama's story late unless he reopens. |
| 3 | Daniel has a job interview. He marks it a **big day**; Ama gets the evening-before card ("…tomorrow at 15:00 your time") and the "Ask how it went" card. Love map prompts land (stressors domain). | Delight: the care loop is the single best responsiveness surface in the app and it works across zones. |
| 4 | A fight by text at 23:40 Accra / 16:40 Vancouver about "you never ask about my day". Ama drafts a hurt message and uses **Help me say this**. Daniel, confused by one of her messages, uses **Understand**. | Delight: translator and Understand both fit the moment. Friction: read receipts show Ama "read at 23:52, no reply", which is the exact anxiety surface LDR research warns about; no opt-out exists. |
| 5 | **Visit.** They use Map story pins in Cape Coast, the Date nights category ("What we loved"), timeline moments. Both forget the app for days, which is correct. | Delight: Story pins become a place-based memory. Nothing nags them for not opening the app (no streak). |
| 6 | **Post-reunion dip.** Daniel flies home. Ama logs a conflict moment (private). Both feel the flatness. Nothing in the app names it. | Fail: the app has a countdown *to* reunion but nothing *after*. The dip is predictable and un-addressed. |
| 7 | Ama opens Pulse: "Refresh" recomputes, score moves, with no explanation she can act on. Weekly check-in exists but no reminder ever came (stub). | Friction: Pulse without the cron is a dead gauge. |
| 8 | Daniel proposes "Our plan" in Planning as a Goal: "Decide by March whether Ama applies for the Canadian study permit." Target date, Steps. | Delight: Goals' "Why this matters to us" is the right frame. Fail: nothing prompts it; they found it by accident. |

### (b) Failures, frustrations, gaps, harms

- **No reunion or post-reunion content.** The product's LDR layer is entirely anticipatory (countdown, dual clock, finder). Stafford & Merolla (02 §9.1) place the risk at reunion, not distance. Nothing acknowledges "the week after they leave".
- **No "our plan" concept for uncertain end dates.** "Will it ever end?" (02 §9.1, [XS]) found uncertainty about *whether* the distance ends is more corrosive than a long distance. The Goals template can hold it, but it's not offered, and the Next-visit template has no "review date for the arrangement".
- **Read receipts are mandatory** (03 §2.2: read-receipt opt-out *absent*). For a 7-zone couple, "read, no reply" at bedtime is a nightly micro-wound. 02 §9.3 explicitly says default these off and make them mutual.
- **Stories realtime not wired** (03 ⚠), so the lowest-pressure LDR surface is also the laggiest.
- **The dormant chat streak counter** (`chat_streaks` flag on, computed, not rendered). If anyone ever renders it, time zones will break it exactly as Paired's 60→2 streak did (01 §2.1). It should be removed, not left loaded.
- **Pulse cron paused; check-in reminder a stub.** The weekly ritual the master spec promised ("thirty seconds on a Sunday", SOUL Principle 6) does not fire.
- **Map and widget are not yet pushed** (`db push` pending; device checks pending). This archetype is the one that most wants them.
- **No quiet hours implementation** (PRINCIPLES §C says never 22:00–08:00; 03 says absent). With a 7-hour gap, an 08:00 Accra daily summary is fine, but a date-poll push "at most hourly" from Daniel's afternoon lands at Ama's 01:00.

### (c) What the evidence says would help

- Shared plan / end-date certainty: "Our plan" card with next visit, a *review date* for the arrangement, and shared goals; non-judgemental when no end date exists (02 §9.2, rank 19, [XS], Moderate).
- Reunion transition module: routines, alone time, space, chores, money; normalise bumpiness (02 §9.2, rank 21, [LONG+XS]).
- Co-presence that shares the mundane and reduces idealisation: open-line video, cook-the-same-recipe, watch-together (02 §9.1 Neustaedter & Greenberg, [QUAL]).
- Assurances and positivity are the maintenance behaviours that most predict satisfaction (Stafford & Canary, [XS]).
- Anticipatory relational savouring buffered separation distress (Froidevaux & Borelli 2023, [LONG]).
- Constant-contact indicators (online, last-seen, read receipts) fuel anxiety and surveillance; default off, mutual (02 §9.3).

### (d) Market

LDR is the most enthusiastic segment (880 mentions, 89% positive; 01 §4). They love distance widgets, countdowns, dual presence, daily rituals that replace dinner talk, "doesn't ask you to always be on your phone" (Couple Joy). They hate time-zone-blind streaks (Paired 60→2; Cozy Couples), widgets that refresh every few days, and messages that "disappear into the abyss". Flamme's bucket list for the next meetup is praised. Requests: meetup planning, countdowns, time zones (01 §3.3 #19).

### (e) Changes

1. **"Our plan" card** — Planning tab, pinned entry created from the Next-visit template or offered once when the couple sets distance in the couple profile. Fields: next visit (date or "not yet"), *review date* ("When will we next talk about the distance?"), one shared goal. Copy: "Couples apart do better with a plan they both can see, even when the end date is open. This is yours to change any time." Never shows elapsed time apart. Data: a Planning entry like any other; no new table.
2. **Reunion module** — Calendar tab. When a calendar event is tagged *reunion* (already used for the countdown), the daily summary on the last day of the visit and the morning after departure carries one line, and the Together hub shows a collapsible card for 7 days: three prompts to answer privately then optionally share ("What did I love about having you here?", "What was harder than I expected?", "One ordinary thing I want to keep doing together from a distance"). Copy, warm and plain: "The days after a visit are often flat. That is normal, not a sign." No AI interpretation; fixed templates. Private-first with explicit "Share with {name}" per answer.
3. **Read receipts: mutual opt-out** — Chat settings. Either partner can turn read receipts off; when one does, neither sees them (symmetric, like map pause). Copy: "Read receipts are off for both of you." No notification to the partner beyond the ticks disappearing. P0 for LDR and for the anxious–avoidant archetype (§4).
4. **Co-presence rituals** — Together rituals templates add "Watch together", "Cook the same thing", "Open line" (a video call left running while doing chores). Template only; no in-app video calling needed. Link previews (oEmbed) already exist for the thing being watched.
5. **Savouring prompt pre-visit** — Love map weekly refresh biases to "dreams" in the 2 weeks before a reunion event; one fixed prompt in the daily summary 3 days before: "What are you most looking forward to?" One push a day is preserved because it rides the existing summary.
6. **Remove the dormant streak counter** — delete the fetch and the flag. It is a time bomb for exactly this couple.
7. **Implement quiet hours** at the engine level (recipient's local 22:00–08:00, deferred not dropped), which the date-poll and forum budgets currently bypass.
8. **Ship the Map and distance widget** (db push + device checks) and **wire Stories realtime**.

## 2. Long-distance with a known move-in date in 4 months

**Efua (London) and Kwame (Kumasi).** Kwame's spousal visa is approved; he lands in London in 16 weeks. GMT/BST, 0–1 hour difference.

### (a) Week by week

| Wk | What they do | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Efua creates **Moving house** from the gallery; Kwame adds a Countdown (16 weeks) and a Budget. They add a Trips entry for a farewell visit to his mother. | Delight: the template fits; the countdown is anticipation. |
| 2 | Home & bills: Efua adds the flat's bills table with "Paid by". Kwame proposes a hand-off: "Register for NHS GP" assigned to himself. | Delight: proposals, not impositions. Friction: bills don't recur (no recurrence engine), so rent is re-entered monthly or not at all. |
| 3 | 36 Questions chapter 3 (future) over three evenings. Sliding Scale: they discover a 7-point gap on "independence". | Delight: blind reveal, then they talk. Fail: Sliding Scale has 6 statements; nothing follows the gap except a Pulse Alignment input they can't see explained. |
| 4 | Kwame worries about money and his mother being alone. He writes in the Journal (deploy-pending) or in Notes. Efua uses Ask Attune Nearby for "things to do in Peckham that cost little" to plan his first weekend. | Delight: Nearby ideas go straight to Planning. Friction: Journal AI reflection is fine but there is no path from "I'm scared" to a conversation. |
| 5–7 | Logistics heavy. Checklists, Expenses (shipping, deposit). Expenses are per-entry pots; they want to see "how much have we spent on the move overall" and can't. | Deliberate by design (D6, D13), but for *one* project, a per-entry total exists already; it is the cross-entry total that is barred. Acceptable. |
| 8 | Efua, anxious about sharing space: "what if we're different in person". No prompt in the app addresses sliding-vs-deciding or cohabitation expectations. | Fail: the transition is the highest-risk moment in this couple's year and the app treats it as a logistics project. |

### (b) Failures and gaps

- **No move-in transition conversation.** Stanley, Rhoades & Markman's sliding-vs-deciding (02 §4.2, [LONG]) and Stafford & Merolla's reunion instability both argue for explicit expectation conversations: chores, alone time, money, families, sex, guests, sleep. The Moving house template is boxes and deposits.
- **No recurrence engine** (Planning v2 non-goal). Bills are the archetypal recurring item; this couple will set up a joint household in the app and discover month two is manual.
- **Sliding Scale is thin (6 statements)** and the only values instrument. A move-in couple needs more than one statement each on money, children, independence, location, ambition, religion.
- **No fairness conversation support.** The division of labour will be renegotiated from zero; the app has assignees and hand-offs but no "how fair does this feel to you" reflection (02 §10.2).
- **Couple type absent.** The app cannot know that "distance ends on 14 Feb" and therefore cannot switch the LDR surfaces off and the cohabiting surfaces on. The reunion countdown will keep counting toward an event that already happened unless they delete it.

### (c) Evidence

- Deciding vs sliding at cohabitation transitions (Stanley et al. 2006, [LONG], Moderate).
- Reunion/transition preparation module (rank 21).
- Making cognitive labour visible and shareable at the point a household forms (Daminger 2019, [QUAL]; Gillespie 2019: fairness over *shared expenses* predicted quality more than housework equity, [XS]).
- Appreciation buffers perceived unfairness (Psych Science 2022).

### (d) Market

Engaged/newlywed and move-in couples want future-planning conversations about money, kids, faith, families (01 §4; "future/engaged/premarital" 236 mentions). Cupla praised for a weekly planning ritual; Honeydue for transparency with choice but hated when data drift "starts fights". Users ask for shared calendars, lists and to-dos *inside* the relationship app (01 §3.3 #7).

### (e) Changes

1. **"Living together" conversation pack** — Planning, offered inside the Moving house template as a block ("Before we share a home"): 8 prompts, each answered privately then revealed side by side (reuse the 36Q reveal mechanic): mornings and sleep, alone time, cleaning standards, guests and family staying over, money flow, phones in bed, how we fight, what home smells like. Copy: "Nothing here is a test. Where you differ is where the conversation is." No scoring; no "alignment %".
2. **Recurring bills** — Planning Home & bills: a minimal monthly/weekly repeat on Table rows or on Checklist items (the Together calendar already has repeats; reuse that engine for a Planning "due" date). Keep "Paid by" per row and no totals per person.
3. **Couple profile: distance state** — `distance: apart | together | mixed` with an optional "distance ends" date. When the date passes, the app proposes (not imposes) retiring the countdown and the Next-visit entry, and offers the Living-together pack. Both partners see the same profile; either can edit; changes are silent (no "Kwame changed the profile" feed).
4. **Expand Sliding Scale** to 30+ statements authored for transitions (chores, money flow, in-law visits, sleep, religion practice at home, children timing), still with "neither / depends" answers (market request #12). Clinical sign-off is already the gate for Sliding Scale content.
5. **Fairness reflection, non-numeric** — Together hub, monthly, private: "This month, how did the split of home things feel to *you*?" with four words (light / fair / heavy / unsure) and an optional sentence, shared only if chosen. Never aggregated, never compared. Copy: "Fairness is a feeling before it is a count."

## 3. Cohabiting 6 years, busy careers, drifting to "roommates"; mental-load imbalance

**Nana and Yaw, Accra.** Both in finance. Nana carries the household's anticipating and monitoring (Daminger's cognitive labour). They are kind, tired, and have not had a conversation that wasn't logistics in months.

### (a) Week by week

| Wk | What they do | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yaw installs Attune "because the calendar looked useful". Together: events me/partner/us; Groceries; Home & bills. Nana enters 14 to-dos. 11 are assigned to "Either". | Delight: proposals and "Either" avoid the nag dynamic. Fail: the "Either" items are the mental load; they are visible to both but Nana is still the one who *noticed* them. |
| 2 | Nana sets a **Date night** ritual (every other week). Yaw accepts. Daily summary 08:00 is the only push and they both like it. | Delight: one push a day, Sunday week-ahead preview. |
| 3 | Chat is logistics. Pulse would show low Connection, but the cron is paused and neither has opened Pulse. Love map prompts arrive (3/week). Nana guesses Yaw's current stressor and is wrong. They laugh. | Delight: the "I didn't know that" moment is the first non-logistics exchange in weeks. Coverage "12 of 60", never accuracy, is the correct restraint. |
| 4 | Yaw is handed "Book the plumber" (proposal). He accepts, forgets. Nana re-proposes. There is no chore history or counter, so there is no ammunition — but also no visibility that this is the third time. | By design. Nana's felt unfairness remains unspoken. |
| 5 | Yaw's promotion comes through. He texts it in chat. Nana replies "nice!!" between meetings. No care card: big-day cards exist only for stressful days. | Fail: the single best-evidenced positive-affect lever (active-constructive responding to good news) has no surface. |
| 6 | Mirror game on a Sunday. Yaw guesses Nana "rested"; she is at 3/10. His private attentiveness flag fires. He feels judged by an app. | Friction: Mirror's flag at 3/4 (03 ⚠ open) is exactly the "scorecard by the back door" the soul forbids. |
| 7 | Nana writes "I feel like staff" in a journal note (deploy-pending). The reflection names a need (recognition). She doesn't know how to raise it. "Help me say this" is in chat, but she isn't drafting a message; she's avoiding one. | Gap: the translator is pull-only and composer-bound; nothing helps someone *start*. |
| 8 | They do the Date night. The Story prompt asks what they'll remember. They write two lines each. | Delight: small, real. |

### (b) Failures and gaps

- **No gratitude / appreciation mechanic** (03 §3: *Absent*; the journal bank deliberately avoids "grateful"). For a roommate drift, appreciation for specific acts is the highest-evidence, cheapest, rule-compatible lever.
- **No share-a-win / capitalisation** (03 §3: care cards cover stress only).
- **No chores template, no recurrence, no "noticing" ownership.** The checklist assigns the *doing*. Daminger's finding is that anticipating and monitoring are the taxing, invisible parts. "Either" is where that load hides.
- **No fairness conversation**; tallies are forbidden (rightly), and nothing non-numeric replaces them.
- **Mirror attentiveness flag** at 3/4 is a private scorecard with no evidence basis; it can only shame.
- **Pulse dead** (cron paused). For a drift couple, the *trend* is the thing; without it the five dimensions are decoration.
- **Quizzes unreachable** (communication, conflict, love-languages). This couple would benefit from "what feels loving to me right now" (02 §7.3), which the love-language quiz could become.
- **Phone-free time**: the app has no "together mode" (02 §10.2) and date night is competing with two phones.

### (c) Evidence

- Specific-act appreciation ("I noticed…"); feeling appreciated → appreciating → responsiveness → commitment (Gordon 2012, [LONG]; Algoe 2010 diary; rank 3, High). Appreciation removes the satisfaction penalty of unfair housework (Psych Sci 2022).
- Active-constructive responding to good news predicts breakups better than responses to bad news (Gable 2006, [LONG]; rank 6).
- Low positive affect in everyday talk predicts *later* divorce (Gottman & Levenson 2000, [LONG]; rank 8). This couple's risk is the slow kind.
- Cognitive labour: ownership of anticipate → decide → do → follow-up as a whole task; fairness conversations, not ledgers (02 §10.2, rank 23).
- Novel shared activity over mundane (Aron 2000, rank 13).
- Phubbing/technoference (XS; rank 27).

### (d) Market

Cohabiting/busy couples value coordination and micro check-ins over deep content; Together (Delony) users describe "living more like coworkers… this helps us" (01 §4). Users ask for shared calendar and lists in the relationship app (#7), "seeing how we've grown" (#11), and "a gentle 'remind my partner' that isn't nagging" (#6). The nag dynamic is hated: Love Nudge "ding reminding your partner to come give you a hug" (01 §2.2). Chore apps outside the corpus are famous for scorekeeping fights; Attune's no-tally rule is a differentiator if it ships with a *feeling*-based alternative.

### (e) Changes

1. **"I noticed…" note** — Chat composer long-press menu and Together hub card. A fixed-format note: "I noticed you {free text}. Thank you." Delivered as an ordinary chat message with a subtle leaf motif, no counter, no history view, no reminder to send one. Optional private "Appreciation jar" (self-facing list of notes *received*, viewable later; never counts). Copy for the empty state: "Specific beats sweeping. 'You remembered my mother's appointment' lands harder than 'you're great'." Data: a message subtype; never enters AI prompts beyond existing per-message tone analysis; never feeds Pulse as a "gratitude rate".
2. **Share a win** — Timeline moments already have *highlight*; add a chat action "Share a win" that posts a highlight and triggers the *same* care-card machinery as big days, inverted: the partner gets an in-app card (never a push) "Ask {name} about it — what was the best part?" opening Chat with a fixed draft. No AI enthusiasm. The partner's reply is theirs.
3. **Household category with recurring chores and whole-task ownership** — Planning starter category "Household": Checklist items with repeat (weekly/monthly), an owner, and a one-line "What owning this means" fixed sub-text ("noticing it needs doing, deciding, doing it, and checking it got done"). Unowned items stay visible to both (D7). No completion counts, no history of who did what (abuse-log rule, 02 §13.3 #9). Hand-off remains a proposal.
4. **Non-numeric fairness reflection** (as §2.5), surfaced quarterly in the hub.
5. **Mirror: remove the attentiveness flag** or raise its bar and make it an *invitation* ("Want to ask {name} how they're actually doing?") rather than a verdict.
6. **Wire the love-languages quiz as "What feels loving to me right now"** — multi-select, changeable, context-tagged (stressed / sad / celebrating). Shown to the partner only if shared. Never used for compatibility (already the rule).
7. **Together mode** — a ritual option "phones away": during a Date night ritual window the app suppresses its own non-safety pushes and shows a single quiet screen. The app practises what it preaches.
8. **Resume the Pulse cron and the check-in reminder** (gated on the existing quality gates). Without a trend, drift is invisible.

## 4. Newly dating (3 months), one more invested; anxious–avoidant pairing

**Adjoa (more invested, anxious) and Kofi (avoidant), Accra.** Adjoa invites Kofi. He accepts after three days. He opens the app every two days.

### (a) Week by week

| Wk | What they do | Where it lands |

Friction: the fallback with no gate reads as "he hasn't joined yet, reflect on that" within the hour. |
| 2 | Adjoa messages a lot. Read receipts show Kofi reading and not replying for hours. She watches the ticks. She uses **Understand** on a two-word reply. The readings are charitable. | Delight: Understand's charitable readings are a genuine antidote to anxious rumination. Harm: read receipts are the pursuer's surveillance tool and the withdrawer's pressure. No opt-out. |

Adjoa reads "anxious–avoidant pairing" copy. | Harm risk: 02 §2.3 and (b) table say attachment labels become accusations. "You're avoidant" is now vocabulary in week 3 of a 3-month relationship. The pairing card conflicts with the other specs' compatibility ban (03 §4.1). |
| 4 | This-or-That (Playful, Romantic). Truth-or-Dare with private skip. Kofi likes games more than talk. | Delight: play works for the less verbal partner (market love #21). The silent skip protects him. |
| 5 | Adjoa proposes a Date night ritual weekly. Kofi declines with a note ("Let's keep it loose for now"). One gentle push back, 7-day quiet expiry. | Delight: declines with dignity are rare in this category. |
| 6 | Adjoa opens Pulse's "Attachment styles" row twice a day. She reads the 9-type description like a horoscope. | Harm: the only reachable quiz is the one most prone to misuse, framed as a type. |

| 8 | They are fine, then not, then fine. Adjoa wonders whether to end it; nothing in the app will tell her (correct). Healing mode exists if she does. | Correct restraint. |

### (b) Failures, frustrations, harms

- **Read receipts mandatory** — the single largest harm vector for this pairing. Pursue–withdraw (Schrodt 2014, [MA], 74 studies) is amplified by any "seen" signal. 02 §13.3 #2: no read receipts by default.
- **Attachment typing as the day-one reward.** The master spec's compatibility preview is "profile-based with explicit labelling"; the built version is an attachment-pairing lookup (03). Labels are dimensional in the science and relationship-specific; a pairing card hands the anxious partner a diagnosis for the avoidant one. CLINICAL's own Ghana calibration for ECR-R avoidance is open (03 §4.2).
- **48-h fallback has no gate and no "keep private / share" prompt on join** (03 PARTIAL). The anxious partner's pre-join reflections could be shared without a deliberate choice.
- **Day-7 pivot missing** (SPEC-ONLY). If Kofi had not joined, Adjoa would be in a waiting state with no dignified exit.
- **No "this is new" content tier.** 36 Questions chapter 3 (future) and Love map "fears" are deep for month three; the market complains about content not matching stage (01 pain #9). There's no couple-stage field to tune this.
- **No soft start for the pursuer and no "stepping back, not away" for the withdrawer** (02 §2.2). The translator helps the pursuer's wording; nothing helps the withdrawer say "I need space and I'm still here".

### (c) Evidence

- Demand/withdraw is reliably harmful and the strongest in distressed couples (Schrodt 2014, [MA], High). The target is the cycle, not the person.

- Partner surveillance tracks anxious attachment (Fox & Warber 2014, [XS]).
- Avoid attachment labels (02 §2.3, (b) table).
- 36 Questions evidence is for *strangers*; in new couples it is a fun escalating deck with skips, not a test (02 §6.5).
- Relationship-awareness content works as well as skills drills (Rogge 2013, [RCT]).

### (d) Market

Ember's 24-hour reply window was called out as "for anxious attachment styles" (pain #19). Autostraddle on Couple: "felt co-dependent in a way we really aren't", "an unreciprocated thumbkiss felt like a bad first kiss" (01 §2.4). Candle's teasing streak nudges were loved by some, which is exactly the mechanic to avoid here. Users want neutral answer options so disagreement isn't a "growth area" (#12).

### (e) Changes

Resolves the compatibility conflict in 03 §4.1 in favour of the stricter specs.
3. **Withdrawer's card** — Chat composer: next to "Help me say this", a one-tap fixed message "I need a bit of space right now. I'm not going anywhere — I'll come back to this." with an optional self-set return time that posts a second fixed message at that time ("I'm back when you are"). User-initiated; no timer visible to the partner; no AI. This is the flooding break (02 §1.3) in its safest form and it is the feature the avoidant partner will actually use.

5. **Stage-aware content** — couple profile `stage: new | established | engaged | married | parents | later-life`. New: Love map biases "history" and "dreams" over "fears"; 36Q chapter 3 is offered later; Scenario prompts skew light.
6. **48-h gate and private/share prompt** — implement the spec: fallback at 48 h, and on partner join: "Keep these private" (default) / "Share". Day-7 pivot as specified.

- **Couple-level shared total per budget period is absent** — it is defensible under D6 only if D6 is read as "no *per-partner* totals". A couple total of the shared pot is not a scoreboard.
- **Append-only expense audit** can become evidence against a partner (02 §13.3 #9: shared features must not become evidence logs). Audit is needed for integrity; its *visibility* should be minimal.
- **Fairness over shared expenses** predicts quality more than housework (Gillespie 2019), and the app has no felt-fairness reflection.
- **Financial coercion** is not in the keyword list or the safety resources.

### (c) Evidence

- Fairness over shared expenses (Gillespie 2019, [XS] n=10k); unfairness to oneself matters most.
- Money dates: periodic guided conversation about values, goals and fears; never force disclosure (02 §12.1, Moderate).
- Approach-motivated giving, no favour ledgers (Impett 2005).
- Shared goals with joint ownership (02 §14.2).
- Honeydue's lagging data "starts fights" — if Attune ever syncs bank data, the same failure follows; stay manual.

### (d) Market

Honeydue: 67% of the sample 1–3★; sync fails "every few days"; "the app starts fights because of miscommunicated information"; loved for reactions and comments on transactions and "choose what you want to share" (01 §2.6, love #25). Honeyfi and Zeta couples are gone. Finance is underserved in the scoping review. Users praise one price for the couple and resent paying to see the partner's side.

### (e) Changes

1. **Money date ritual** — Together rituals template "Money date" (monthly, 30 min) with a fixed agenda card: what came in, what went out (they look at their own statements, not the app), one worry each, one thing to celebrate, one decision. "Memories from this ritual" holds the decision. No numbers stored unless they type them. Copy: "Short, regular and boring beats rare and explosive."
2. **Money values pack** (as §5.3) offered to every couple that creates Home & bills; prompts about history, shame, fear, generosity to family; private-then-reveal.
3. **Shared total per budget period** — Planning Budget block: "Spent from our pot this month" as a couple figure (never per partner). Keep "no per-partner totals" absolute. Copy in the block explains it: "One number for both of you. Attune never adds up who paid what."
4. **Recurring bills** (as §2.2).
5. **Debt as a private-then-shared Goal** — Goals template variant "Pay down": the owner chooses whether the balance is visible to the partner or only the progress bar; the progress celebration still fires at 100%. Never auto-created from expenses. Copy avoids "debt" shame: "Paying something down."
6. **Audit visibility** — keep the append-only ledger for integrity but show it only on explicit "History" tap, with no per-person filter and no export of the audit; exports include current lines only. This is the evidence-log rule applied.
7. **Financial-coercion resources** — Safety Resources: "Money being controlled?" section; add financial-control phrases to the Tier-2 lexicon review.
8. **Fairness reflection** (§2.5) includes "the money side" as one of its four words.

### (b) Failures and gaps

- **No personal dreams board with "how to support me"** (Michelangelo / secure base; 02 §14.2).
- **No share-a-win** (as §3).
- **No savouring prompts** (relive a moment; anticipatory before a trip).

### (c) Evidence

- Self-expansion: novel, mildly challenging joint activities raise relationship quality (Aron 2000, [XS + experiment], rank 13).
- Michelangelo and secure-base support: affirm each other's ideal selves; available, encouraging, *non-interfering* (Drigotas 1999; Feeney 2004; rank 22). Intrusive goal help backfires.
- Capitalisation (rank 6), savouring (rank 28), appreciation (rank 3).
- Positives matter as much as conflict management; low positive affect predicts *later* divorce — happy couples are the ones for whom this is the whole game (02 §0.3 #2).
- Boosters: gains fade without periodic check-ins (Córdova 2014, [RCT]); a strengths-first quarterly check-up is the retention feature with the strongest evidence, and it fits a happy couple best because it is strengths-first.

### (d) Market

Users love "learning new things about a partner after years" (305 reviews), play, and shared memories (940). Evergreen users on "day 300" asked to see "how our relationship has grown" (#11). Flamme's bucket list is praised. Paying couples finished Evergreen's quizzes in 3 weeks (pain #8: thin content). Candle's local date ideas and photo prompts "felt fun and real". The anniversary artefact is shareable *as a story*, not as a score (THESIS §7.2), which is precisely what differentiates it from Paired's "growth area" labelling.

### (e) Changes

1. **Dreams board** — Planning category "Dreams": each partner adds *personal* aspirations with a visibility choice and a support preference ("Cheer me on" / "Help me plan" / "Just know"). The partner sees only the chosen level; no progress tracking by the partner, no reminders to the dreamer from the partner. Copy: "Tell {name} how to be in your corner." Data: owner-only edit; visible per item.
2. **Share a win** (§3.2).
3. **Savouring prompts** — Love map weekly refresh gains a fifth domain "moments": "Relive one moment this month you felt close — what made it?"; and anticipatory savouring 3 days before a Trips entry (rides the daily summary).
4. **"Try something new" challenge** — Together ideas (flip `ai_together_enabled` after the RPC smoke test) plus a non-AI monthly card "Neither of you has done this": drawn from a curated local bank with the sensory/access filters from §12, proposed to both, accepted by both, skippable with no record of skips.
5. **Our year** — the anniversary report, built *without* the paused AI pipeline first: a templated recap from Planning, Timeline, Stories, rituals and Love map coverage ("34 of 60 asked", "7 rituals you kept", "the places on your map"), no scores, no counts of who did what, no comparison. Shareable as an image the couple composes (they pick which cards). AI narrative can come later behind the verdict gates.
6. **Quarterly check-up, strengths-first** — replaces the dead weekly Pulse loop for most couples: each partner privately answers 6 items (strengths first, then "one thing I'd like more of"), sees their own answers, and both can opt in to a *themes-only* joint view (no numbers, no deltas, no per-person attribution), followed by one "Ask each other" prompt. Fits the no-couple-report rule because it combines nothing but the two people's *chosen* shared themes. Copy: "A check-up, not a score."
7. **Couple profile `intent`** to bias the hub: *grow* surfaces Dreams, challenges, savouring; *reconnect* surfaces "I noticed…", rituals, the check-up; *navigating a change* surfaces the relevant transition pack.

### B. Features that serve many archetypes at once

1. **Couple profile** (gap 2). Fields, each with a privacy class:
   - *Shared, both edit, silent changes*: `stage` (new / established / engaged / married / parents / later-life), `distance` (apart / together / mixed, optional end date), `intent` (grow / reconnect / navigating a change), `household` (just us / with family / with children, ages banded).
   - *Per partner, self-set, partner sees only "set"*: `faith_content` (none / tradition / prefer not), `sexual_content` (off / on), `health_context` (none / caregiving / illness — private), `communication_preferences` (the "How I communicate" card, shared by choice), `access_needs` (sensory / motor / vision tags that filter ideas; never labelled as a diagnosis).
   - *Rules*: special-category values (faith, orientation-adjacent, health) are stored per user under `auth.uid()` RLS, never inferred from messages or games, excluded from all AI prompts except where the user's own question carries them, exportable and deletable, cleared on unlink, and never surfaced on widgets, pushes or the calendar mirror. Shared surfaces that depend on two per-partner gates (intimacy lists, devotion rituals) appear only when both are on and ask fresh mutual opt-in per session. Nothing in the profile produces a score, a type or a compatibility statement.
2. **Private-then-reveal conversation packs** (gap 5), one mechanic (already shipped in 36Q and Love map) reused for: living together, reunion, our families, money values, interfaith, newborn, caregiving. Fixed prompts; no AI; each answer shared individually by choice; "where you differ is where the conversation is" copy; no "growth area" labels.
3. **"I noticed…" appreciation note and "Share a win" with the ask-about-it card** (gap 3). Reuses chat message subtypes and the existing big-day care machinery. Serves 9 archetypes.
4. **"Space, not distance" card + return time** (gap 7). One fixed message, user-initiated, self-set timer, second fixed message on return. Serves pursue–withdraw, flooding, ADHD overwhelm, new-parent exhaustion and money fights alike without the app ever detecting escalation (keeps the "never automatic" rule and avoids the Month-5 "live cooldown banner" tension noted in 03).
5. **Quarterly strengths-first check-up** (gaps 6, 14) as the booster the evidence supports, with a themes-only consented joint view. It gives the paused Pulse pipeline a purpose it can serve without weekly crons and without numbers.
6. **Safety floor completion** (gap 4): production lexicon with DV review (adding outing-threat and financial-control classes), country routing with international fallback and emergency numbers, PIN and quick exit everywhere, journal keyword net, self-harm resources, neutral icon. Every archetype benefits; two depend on it.
7. **Read receipts default-off for the first 90 days and mutual opt-out; engine-level quiet hours; delete the dormant streak** (gap 1). Zero new UI beyond one settings row.
8. **Recurrence** (bills, chores, self-set reminders) — one engine (the calendar's) reused in Planning and Reminders. Serves 2, 3, 8, 10, 12, 13.
9. **Family relation + side + per-event tag** (gap 12), already in the schema; serves 5, 6, 8, 10, 11.
10. **"Our year" templated recap** — non-AI first, from data the couple already made. Serves 10 and 14 directly and is the thesis's named growth artefact.

### E. What this document does not recommend (and why)

- Any partner-facing count, ratio, trend comparison or "growth area" label. Every archetype that could benefit from a number is also one where the number becomes a weapon.
- Automatic conflict detection banners (the Month-5 "live cooldown"). The user-initiated pause card gets most of the value with none of the misfire risk.
- Partner-controlled reminders or "remind my partner" buttons, however gentle. The market asks for them (#6) and the evidence (Love Nudge, Feeney's intrusive support, 02 §12.7) says they curdle. Proposals and the daily summary already carry the legitimate case.
- Bank sync for money. Honeydue's 234 sync complaints and "starts fights" are the whole argument.
- A companion-style AI for any of these couples, including the lonely LDR partner at 02:00 and the postpartum mother. Decision 28 stands; the right responses are fixed resources, the commons, and the partner.
- Faith or sexual content shown by default to anyone, or inferred from anything.
