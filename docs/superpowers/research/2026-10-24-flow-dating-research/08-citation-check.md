- 01 `[UNVERIFIED]`;

It also covers the priority items the programme lead named: Ghana bill status, the legal status of high-risk countries, age-assurance law, the data-protection statutes, nine key science papers and six product facts. The researchers' files were not edited.

## How this was checked

- **Web search** (about 65 queries) and **page fetches** for law, news and product facts. Primary pages were used where reachable: ILGA, Human Dignity Trust, Grindr, Ofcom, FTC, Google Play policy, the App Store listing, the eSafety Commissioner, the FIDO Alliance, Pew's own topline PDF and the Pulse GH and Citi reports.
- **Bibliographic APIs** for papers: Crossref (citations and many abstracts), PubMed E-utilities (abstracts), Semantic Scholar (abstracts and TLDRs) and the arXiv API. A citation counts as **verified** only when the finding itself was seen, in an abstract or the full text. Where only the citation could be confirmed, the row says "citation confirmed" and the verdict is **Still unverified**.
- **Blocked:** pewresearch.org HTML (403 and JavaScript challenge), JURIST, Springer and Nature landing pages, the eharmony.ca page, Medium, and Kenya's and Ghana's statute PDFs (scanned, so not machine-readable here). Secondary sources were used instead and are named as such.
- **Verdicts:** each row gets exactly one:
  - **Verified:** a source supports the claim as written. Small nuances are noted.
  - **Corrected:** the claim is partly wrong; the right version is given.
  - **Contradicted:** a good source says the opposite.
  - **Still unverified:** no adequate source was reached, or only the citation was confirmed.
- **No legal advice.** The statute rows confirm what secondary legal sources say. Counsel should still read the gazetted texts.

**Totals (166 rows):** Verified 96 · Corrected 19 · Contradicted 1 · Still unverified 50.
