# 02 — Watching stories (the reel)

Reviewer 2 of 6 · 2026-10-23 · Scope: `story_reel_screen.dart` (read in full), `story_progress_bars.dart`, `story_providers.dart` / `story_read_repository.dart` (as the reel consumes them), view/seen/reply migrations, `message_bubble.dart` story branch, map/calendar entry points.

Evidence method: code trace, plus throwaway widget probes run from the scratchpad (never in the repo) against the real `StoryReelScreen` and its real providers with a fake gateway. Probe results are quoted where they confirm a finding. Repo suites run: `flutter test test/features/stories/story_reel_test.dart test/features/stories/story_reply_test.dart` (25/25 pass). On `attune_spec3a`: `story_seen_state_contracts.sql`, `story_reply_contracts.sql` and `story_rpc_contracts.sql` all held.

## 1. Verdict

The server side of watching is solid. The view RPC is idempotent, refuses the author and expired items, keeps the exact view time private, and hides seen-state when the couple has it off. The reply trigger cannot be tricked into quoting another couple's story. The reel's own sequencing code is careful in places: a generation counter guards against late callbacks, the two pause causes (holding and backgrounding) are kept separate, and the video player is disposed on every transition. **But the reel does not meet the standard yet, and one path loses data.**

The central defect is that the reel tracks **position in a list**, not **which story is on screen**, while that list is refetched underneath it. Any refresh can change it: a deletion, a new post, app resume, or the viewer's own first view while seen-state is on. After a refresh, the delete button, the reply composer and the progress bars can all point at a different story from the one being shown. Deleting from the calendar reel then deleting again **permanently removes a story the person never saw** (confirmed by probe).

Every one of those refreshes also blanks the reel to a spinner. The photo timer never stops at the end of an item. So when the last photo finishes, the reel closes **the screen beneath it as well** (chat, calendar, map), and it dismisses any confirm dialog that is open.

A first item that fails to load re-tries **once per frame, forever**. A view is marked "seen" before the photo's bytes have arrived. Preloading never hits the cache. The reel always restarts at the oldest item rather than the first one you haven't seen.

Against Snapchat, Instagram and WhatsApp, the reel today is not fast, not reliable, and not fluid.

## 2. What is solid (with evidence)

- **Late-callback safety.** Every post-await write checks `mounted && generation == _generation` (`story_reel_screen.dart:404,440,466-469,473,708,762`). The same-frame double-advance race is closed and tested (`raceAdvanceForTest`, `:733-738`).
- **Pause model.** Holding and backgrounding are separate flags ORed into `_paused` (`:273-292,615-647`). `AppLifecycleListener` pauses on inactive/hide/pause and resumes on resume (`:342-347,357-381`). A video that ends while backgrounded starts the next item paused (`:482-486`). Covered by tests (`story_reel_test.dart:793-935`).
- **Video lifecycle.** The old player is disposed on every item change (`:398-402`), a player that finishes initialising after the person has moved on is disposed (`:466-469`), and everything is torn down in `dispose()` (`:1019-1028`). End-of-clip detection latches so it fires once (`:130-140`). video_player 2.10.1 seeks to the end on completion, so `position >= duration` is reached.
- **Buffering stalls the bar.** Video progress is read from `player.position` (`:584-587`), so the bar stops while the player buffers.
- **Server view contract.** `mark_story_viewed` locks the row, then gives one identical refusal for missing, deleted, own, expired and not-a-member (`20261017140000_story_seen_state.sql` mark function). The insert is `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. The signal bump only happens while seen receipts are effective. `story_views` has no client grants and is not read by the export. The list RPCs hide the author-facing flag when seen receipts are off. `story_items` is not in the realtime publication, so the boolean cannot leak through Realtime. Contract file held on the scratch DB.
- **Replies are trusted server-side.** The trigger requires a live story in the same active, unarchived relationship and a member sender, sets `quoted_text` itself, and uses one generic error (`20260939010000_story_replies.sql:63-112`). The client never sends `quoted_text` (`supabase_chat_repository.dart:547-562`). Contract file held.
- **Tap-through availability uses RLS.** `getReplyTarget` is a plain RLS read (`story_read_repository.dart:560-571`). Null shows one generic "Story no longer available" message (`message_bubble.dart:96-112`). Expired-but-kept stories open through the day reel, as spec §5.4 requires.
- **Signed URLs** are 600 s, minted fresh per item and never persisted (`story_read_repository.dart:410-415,573-584`). The bucket policy requires a live row (`20260940120000:55`), so a deleted story stops signing immediately.
- **Image decode size** is bounded upstream: capture caps the longest edge at 1600 px and the file at 800 KB (`chat_image_preparer.dart:224-227`), so a full decode in the reel is fine.
- **Screen-reader actions** exist for next, previous and dismiss (`:1551-1575`), and each item change is announced (`:430-435`).

## 5. Standards gap (what Snapchat / Instagram / WhatsApp do that a person would expect here)

1. **Resume at the first unseen story**, with seen segments pre-filled (#8). The single biggest behavioural difference.
2. **Instant next item**: preload the next one or two items, video included, and keep the frame on screen until the next is ready (#7). No spinner between items on a normal connection.
3. **Owner actions in the viewer**: delete your own story (#9) and see "Seen" on it (#10). All three apps also let you **save your own story** to the camera roll. Attune has no save/download for the author. Low-cost, and expected for "memories".
4. **A header with the posting time** ("3h", or "Tue 14:02" in the calendar) and **whose story it is**. The day and map reels mix both partners with no author cue at all.
5. **The reply opens the exact story** it quotes (#11), and the chat quote shows a **live thumbnail** while the story exists (spec §5.4 allows it; currently text only).
6. **Video controls**: a mute/sound toggle, plus a buffering indicator (#15).
7. **Hold to pause hides the chrome** (bars, buttons, composer) for an unobstructed view. Here the overlay stays.
8. **Swipe-down follows the finger**, with the reel shrinking and the background showing through, and snaps back if released early. Here it is a threshold jump with no visual tracking. **Swipe up focuses the reply.**
9. **Keyboard etiquette**: tapping outside dismisses the keyboard without navigating, and the reel stays paused until the composer is closed (#12).
10. **Tap left on the first item restarts it** (#27). With two authors, **swiping horizontally between "mine" and "partner's"** (or continuing to the partner's reel after mine) is a smaller gap worth considering. Today each ring is its own dead-end reel.

(Reactions and view lists are deliberately out of scope per spec §7 and are not counted as gaps.)
