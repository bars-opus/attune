# 03 — Story rings row and keeping it fresh

Reviewer 3 of 6 · 2026-10-23 · Area: `story_rings_row.dart`, `story_ring.dart`, `story_providers.dart`, `story_read_repository.dart`, Conversations screen placement, read/refresh RPCs and the realtime signal.

## 1. Verdict

The server side of the rings is sound. Reads go through SECURITY DEFINER RPCs that filter on membership and expiry, the realtime signal is a refetch trigger with no data in it, it is now in the publication, and the ring never computes expiry on the device, so device clock skew and timezone cannot move a story on or off the ring. The client contract mostly holds too: my ring is always drawn, an empty partner ring draws nothing, a posting ring and a failed-post notice exist, viewing refreshes the viewer's ring, and resume triggers a refetch. All 23 ring tests and 23 read-repository tests pass.

It does not yet meet the Instagram/WhatsApp bar for "fast, reliable, fluid":

- **Tapping my own story opens the wrong screen.** The `+` badge's 48dp hit box covers the centre of my ring, so tapping my own story opens the camera instead of my reel. I confirmed this with a probe test. The existing test misses it because that test never wires the camera callback.
- **The open reel blanks on every change signal.** Any signal sets the shared pager back to plain `loading`, so the open reel (and the day view) flashes to a spinner and the reply box loses focus. That includes the viewer's own first view when seen-receipts are on.
- **The ring is stale or wrong at four common moments:**
  - right after a post finalizes (it flashes back to `+`)
  - when a story expires while the app stays open (nothing refetches)
  - on a cold start offline (it shows "Add to your story" and no partner ring, with no cache)
  - after a failed URL signing (a grey circle that sticks)
- **Ring thumbnails are cached under the signed URL instead of the storage key.** So they re-download after every PIN unlock or cold start, crowd chat photos out of the shared disk cache, and a deleted story's thumbnail stays on disk for up to 30 days.

Counts: Critical 0 · High 2 · Medium 6 · Low 7.

## 2. What is solid (with evidence)

- **My ring is always drawn; an empty partner ring draws nothing.** The map shape plus the `summaries[myId]` lookup (`story_rings_row.dart:140-190`) and the row-absent RPC contract (`20261017140000_story_seen_state.sql:98-127`) do this. Tests: `story_rings_test.dart` "my ring is drawn unconditionally" and "empty renders nothing".
- **Server-authoritative expiry.** `expires_at = created_at + interval '24 hours'` is a CHECK in `20260938010000_stories_schema.sql:61`. The ring filters `si.expires_at > now()` on the server (`20261017140000…:125`). No client date maths, so the device clock, timezone and DST cannot misplace a story on the ring.
- **Authorization on the ring read.** `get_story_ring_summary` is SECURITY DEFINER and repeats `deleted_at IS NULL AND story_relationship_is_open(p_relationship_id, auth.uid())` (`20261017140000…:123-124`). A paused, ended or archived relationship returns nothing. The signal table's SELECT policy uses the same predicate (`20260938020000_stories_rls.sql:44-46`), and only SELECT is granted (`20260938100000…:48-53`).
- **Realtime is wired and safe.** The publication was added (`20261018130500_story_signals_realtime.sql`). The payload is dropped (`story_read_repository.dart:580-582`). Every (re)subscribe emits a catch-up refetch (`:584-594`), and so does app resume (`story_providers.dart:86-90`). Sources that bump the signal:
  - finalize (`20260938120000…:283`)
  - delete (`20260938140000…:158`)
  - archive swap (`20260938090000…:598`)
  - places (`20261017100000…:131,143`)
  - first view, only while seen-receipts are on (`20261017140000…:181-183`)
- **Seen flip on the viewer's phone.** It does not depend on the signal: `story_reel_screen.dart:673-676` invalidates the ring summary after each successful `markViewed`. `story_seen_state_contracts.sql` passed on `attune_spec3a` ("all held").
- **Background refresh keeps the last value.** The ring is a `FutureProvider`, so a signal-driven rerun keeps the previous value (Riverpod 2.6.1 `asyncTransition`), and a failed refresh keeps the last map. Only the first load can come back empty (finding M3).
- **Notifications fire on every signal, not just on change.** I checked Riverpod 2.6.1 `FutureHandlerProviderElementMixin.handleUpdateShouldNotify`: data→data always notifies. So repeated `AsyncData(null)` signals do re-run the ring.
- **Lifecycles.** The ring, signal and URL providers are all `.autoDispose`. The family dispose closes the channel (`story_providers.dart:69,92-96`) and the gateway dispose closes all channels (`:56`).
- **PIN lock.** `SafetyLockGate` returns `PinLockScreen` instead of the app while locked (`safety_lock_gate.dart:72-76`), so the rings are unmounted and nothing is fetched or painted while locked.
- **Sign-out.** It clears the in-memory `ImageCache` and empties `DefaultCacheManager` (`auth_provider.dart:191-207`, `chat_image_cache.dart:108-115`), which is where ring thumbnails live.
- **Rendering.**
  - Capped at 12 segments, solid beyond that (`story_ring.dart:142-144`).
  - A seen segment is faded *and* thinner, so the state is not shown by colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1, `:582,589`).
  - The unviewed count is clamped against a server value that drifts (`story_rings_row.dart:269`).
  - Reduce-motion stops the pending ring's animation (`story_ring.dart:434-439`).
  - Light and dark goldens exist.
  - The ring and the `+` are separate semantics nodes with a 48dp hit box for the `+`.
  - A failed post gets a visible Retry/Discard notice (`story_rings_row.dart:342-380`), with each button reachable by a screen reader (probe: the buttons stay as separate child nodes).
  - A capture for a different relationship never paints this ring (`:130`), and when several are queued the newest wins (`:135`).

## 5. Standards gap (Instagram / Snapchat / WhatsApp, two-person relevant)

1. **Tapping your own ring plays your story.** Only the small `+` opens the camera (H1).
2. **Resume at the first unseen item.** Instagram and WhatsApp open a partly-seen ring at the first new item. Attune always opens at the oldest (spec §5.5 mandates this), so with 4 seen and 1 new you tap through 4 old items. Recommend: start at `items.indexWhere(!hasBeenViewed)`, keeping the seen ones reachable with a back tap.
3. **Cached tray offline and instant on launch.** The tray paints from cache before the network; Attune paints `+` and no partner ring (M3).
4. **Thumbnails stay put between visits.** No placeholder flash after unlock or relaunch (M4).
5. **Expiry is live.** The ring drops a story when it expires, even with the app open (M2).
6. **The posting tile becomes the posted story seamlessly** (M1), and the local pending item is viewable before upload completes (L1).
7. **Name labels under the rings** ("Your story" / "Ama"). Attune deliberately shows no label (§5.1), so the only cue for which ring is whose is position plus the `+`. For a two-ring row this is fine visually, but a short label would help large-text and low-vision users. Optional, owner decision.
8. **Unread-count accessibility** ("3 new") (L2).
9. **A queued post retries automatically when connectivity returns** instead of giving up after about 2 minutes of backoff. The outbox has no connectivity listener (this belongs to the outbox reviewer; it shows up here as a premature "could not be posted" notice).
