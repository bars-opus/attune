# 01 — Posting a story, end to end (capture → prep → outbox → intent/upload/finalize)

Reviewer 1 of 6 · 2026-10-23 · read-only review of the working tree at `694150c1`, uncommitted edits included.

## 1. Verdict

The server half of posting is strong. Finalize is idempotent under real concurrency. Every server-owned field is derived on the server. Upload keys come from intents that only their owner can use. Size and MIME are checked against `storage.objects`. Cleanup goes through the deletion queue and is idempotent. The client half does not yet meet the bar the chat pipeline now meets in this repo, or the Snapchat/Instagram/WhatsApp standard:
- The outbox gives up on a network outage after about 30 s and never resumes on its own.
- It uploads through the SDK with a flat 30 s total timeout that never aborts the request. The retry then collides with its own orphaned upload (409) and gets misread as a network error.
- Any Storage refusal (expired intent after the app was in the background, a missing file) is treated as a network error.
- Intents live only in memory, so a kill mid-post always costs an attempt.
- Files sit in purgeable `tmp/` under absolute paths, and nothing purges them on sign-out, which the spec requires.

The capture side has its own problems:
- A 640×480 sensor preset makes photos and video 0.3 MP.
- A full 60 s take is very likely rejected by the exact 60 s prepare ceiling.
- A denied microphone blocks photos too.
- There is no review step: a tap posts straight to the partner.

Verdict: **not yet robust**. There are 7 High, 8 Medium and 7 Low findings, and no Critical.

## 2. What is solid (with evidence)

- **Idempotent finalize, three layers.**
  - The `(author_id, client_story_id)` lookup runs before any intent check (`20260938050000_stories_finalize_rpc.sql:86-92`).
  - `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` covers two simultaneous replays (`:233-245`).
  - The race fix re-runs the lookup when a locked intent is already used (`20260938120000_stories_finalize_race_fix.sql:31-42`).
  - `clientStoryId` is minted once at capture (`story_camera_screen.dart:214`) and never regenerated (`story_outbox_controller.dart:10-16`).
  - Test: "a dropped finalize response does not post twice" (`story_outbox_controller_test.dart:330`).
  - `story_rpc_contracts.sql` passes on `attune_spec3a`.
- **Server-derived fields.** `author_id`, `media_type`, both keys, `expires_at` and `occurred_on` are computed on the server (`20260938050000:223-232`). The offset is clamped to ±840 (`:208`).
- **Intent and storage authorization.**
  - Keys are random, relationship-scoped and expire in 15 minutes (`20260938040000_stories_intent_rpc.sql:182-185`).
  - INSERT is allowed only for the caller's own unused, unexpired key, through a SECURITY DEFINER predicate that keeps the intent table free of client grants (`20260940120000_story_storage_policy_isolation.sql:13-43`).
  - There are no UPDATE or DELETE policies (`20260938030000:151-153`).
  - Size and MIME are checked from `storage.objects` against each intent's own `max_bytes` (`20260938050000:164-194`).
  - Abuse bounds are 20 open intents and 120 calls per hour (`20260938040000:159-180`).
- **Orphan cleanup.** It runs hourly (`20260938090000:659-663`) and is idempotent because each row is stamped. It deletes through `media_deletion_queue` and the Storage API, never with direct SQL, and never queues a used key (`:62-129`). `story_maintenance_contracts.sql` passes.
- **Outbox at rest.**
  - The payload is encrypted with AES-GCM using the keystore key (`story_outbox_store.dart:146-163`).
  - The store fails closed, and `put` returns false, which the camera surfaces (`story_camera_screen.dart:237-245`).
  - FIFO order is pinned to the first-insert `created_at` (`story_outbox_backend_io.dart:85-95`).
  - Permanent failures stay visible with Retry and Discard (`story_rings_row.dart:340-380`).
- **Metadata privacy.**
  - The native image path drops EXIF and the Dart fallback clears it (`capture_image_preparer.dart:140, 166`). `capture_image_preparer_exif_test.dart` covers GPS and orientation.
  - MP4 location atoms are stripped (`chat_video_preparer.dart:309`).
  - MIME is sniffed from magic bytes, and there is a decompression-bomb cap (`capture_image_preparer.dart:107-119`).
- **Pin to our map.**
  - Off by default, auto-off near home, and never prompts without a tap (`story_pin_controller.dart:19-24, 98-102, 151-157`).
  - The lookup is bounded at 20 s fix plus 10 s geocode (`story_place_resolver.dart:68-90`).
  - The place is written only on a fresh insert, behind its flag, and is never fatal to the post (`20261017100000_story_places.sql:78-99`).
  - Places are purged when the relationship closes (`:182-192`).
- **Tests.**
  - 48/48 Flutter tests pass across `story_outbox_controller`, `story_outbox_store`, `story_camera`, `capture_camera`, `story_repository`, `story_outbox_place` and `capture_image_preparer_exif`.
  - `story_places_contracts.sql` passes once the migration's own REVOKEs are re-applied (see finding 22).

## 5. Standards gap (two-person private story, posting only)

What a person used to Snapchat, Instagram or WhatsApp would expect, and Attune lacks:

1. **Preview before send, with Retake** (finding 7). All three show the shot and require a deliberate Send.
2. **Full-screen, high-resolution capture**: 9:16 framing, 1080p video and full-sensor photos (finding 5). Attune captures 4:3 at 480p.
3. **Upload that survives connectivity and backgrounding.** Instagram and WhatsApp keep posting when the signal returns and continue briefly in the background (iOS `beginBackgroundTask`, Android WorkManager). Attune gives up after about 30 s offline (finding 1), has no background continuation, and resumes only from Conversations (finding 9).
4. **Real progress and Cancel on the pending item** (finding 13). Instagram shows a byte-accurate bar and lets you discard while it is posting.
5. **Post from the camera roll.** All three let you share an existing photo or video as a story. Attune is camera-only (the chat picker and preparers already exist).
6. **Capture controls**: flash or front flash, pinch and slide-to-zoom while recording, and a double-tap to flip. Only flip exists.
7. **Light creative layer**: a caption or text sticker at minimum. Drawing and filters are optional for this audience.
8. **Works without a microphone**: photos and silent video when the mic is denied (finding 6).
9. **Clear failure reasons**, such as "Couldn't post — no connection, we'll retry" versus "Too long" (finding 20).
