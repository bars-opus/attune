## 1. WHAT ATTUNE IS

Attune is a relationship intelligence system.

Not a chat app. Not a quiz app. Not a couples journal. Not a dating app.
A system that builds a compounding, specific, non-transferable understanding
of a specific relationship over time — and surfaces that understanding
back to the people in that relationship as clarity.

The product has a chat system, quizzes, games, a pulse score, a forum,
and a dating mode. These are the surfaces through which intelligence is
built and delivered. They are not the product. The intelligence is the product.

Every message a couple sends builds the pattern memory.
Every conflict session that resolves or fails updates the analysis engine.
Every game played enriches the compatibility profile.
Every week of pulse scores adds to the longitudinal picture.

Users do not have to do extra work to build this intelligence.
They have to do the thing they would do anyway — be in their relationship.
The intelligence accumulates as a byproduct of living.

That is what Attune is.

## 2. WHAT ATTUNE IS NOT

**Not a therapist.**
Attune surfaces patterns. It does not diagnose. It does not treat.
It does not tell people what their relationship means or what to do about it.
Labels belong to clinicians. Attune observes and reflects.

**Not a judge.**
Attune never tells users whether their relationship is good or bad,
healthy or unhealthy, worth staying in or leaving.
That decision belongs entirely to the people in the relationship.
See Section 2a for the precise definition of "verdict" as used in this product.

**Not a surveillance tool.**
The AI analyses behaviour to help people understand themselves,
not to build a case against a partner.
Asymmetric behavioural data is self-facing only — always.

**Not a game.**
Attune is designed to be worth returning to, not designed to make
leaving feel painful. The distinction is subtle but it is everything.

**Not a social media platform.**
The forum is anonymous text. The product is private by design.
No relationship data becomes social content. No couple's health
is visible to anyone outside that couple.

**Not ReelShort. Not Tinder.**
These are the anti-products. See Section 11.

### 2a. What "Verdict" Means in Attune

The word "verdict" is used deliberately in this product as the name
for the monthly relationship summary. It is kept because it is honest —
users are asking for clarity, and clarity is what Attune delivers.

However, "verdict" in Attune means one specific thing and must never
mean anything else:

**A verdict in Attune is a sourced pattern summary.**

It is: here is what the data shows, here is which data it came from,
here is one thing worth attending to.

It is never: a judgment on the relationship's worth, a score that
ranks the relationship as good or bad, a recommendation about whether
to stay or leave, or a clinical assessment of either person's psychology.

The verdict is clarity about patterns. What those patterns mean for
the relationship is entirely the user's decision to make. Attune
delivers the summary. The user delivers the meaning.

This distinction must be reflected in every verdict headline, every
insight card, and every piece of copy generated by the product.
A verdict that reads like a judge has failed. A verdict that reads
like a wise, attentive friend sharing what they noticed has succeeded.

## 3. THE CORE BELIEF

Most people repeat the same relationship mistakes for years
because they have no objective data, no pattern recognition,
and no shared language for what is actually happening between them.

Attune exists to change that.

The product's deepest belief is that **understanding precedes change**.
A person who can see their pursue-withdraw pattern in data is not
the same person they were before they saw it. A couple who can name
the recurring unmet need beneath their recurring fight is not the same
couple they were before they named it. Understanding is not passive.
It is the precondition for every genuine change that follows.

This belief connects to Carl Jung's concept of bringing the shadow
into consciousness, to Carl Rogers' conviction that people grow when
genuinely seen, and to Viktor Frankl's insight that meaning — not
pleasure or relief — is the deepest human motivation.

Attune's job is to produce that understanding.
What people do with it is entirely their own.

## 4. THE PSYCHOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK

Attune's design is grounded in four research-backed frameworks.
Every feature should be evaluable against at least one of them.

### 4.1 Self-Determination Theory — Ryan and Deci

Humans are intrinsically motivated when three psychological needs are met:
**autonomy** (feeling in control of one's own choices), **competence**
(feeling effective and capable), and **relatedness** (feeling connected
and belonging to others).

**How this maps onto Attune:**

Autonomy is satisfied when the product surfaces patterns and steps back —
never telling users what to decide, always respecting their capacity to
choose. Every verdict, every insight, every recommendation is framed as
information, not instruction.

Competence is satisfied when the communication score goes up, when Mirror
game accuracy improves, when the translator reveals a clearer way to
express something. The product measures real skill development and makes
that development visible over time.

Relatedness is satisfied by the shared pulse score, the games played
together, the compatibility preview, the forum community — the feeling
of being known and knowing someone.

**The critical failure mode to avoid:**
Research shows most behaviour change apps use SDT to make the technology
engaging rather than to help users genuinely internalise the target
behaviour. The target behaviour for Attune is not "open the app."
It is "communicate better, understand yourself more honestly, grow
in your relationship." Every design decision must ask: does this help
users internalise better relationship behaviour, or does it just make
the app more engaging to open?

### 4.2 The Fogg Behavior Model — B = MAP

Behavior happens when **Motivation**, **Ability**, and a **Prompt**
converge at the same moment. The model's central insight: increasing
Ability — making the behaviour easier — is almost always more effective
than increasing Motivation. Motivation fluctuates. Simplicity is stable.

**How this maps onto Attune:**

Every feature must be designed for the user's lowest-ability moment.
After a conflict. During a difficult week. When someone is flooded,
exhausted, or avoiding. If a feature only works when people feel good,
it does not work when it matters most.

The weekly check-in is three taps maximum. The conflict translator is
one button in the composer. The safety resources are one tap from anywhere.
These are not just UX decisions. They are Fogg compliance — making the
right behaviour possible at the hardest moment.

Prompts are not interchangeable. A notification at 10pm Sunday when a
user is relaxed requires a different design than a notification after
a conflict is detected on Tuesday night. The type of prompt must match
where the user sits on the Motivation-Ability graph at the moment it fires.

### 4.3 The Hook Model — Nir Eyal

Habit-forming products create a four-stage loop: Trigger, Action,
Variable Reward, Investment. The goal is to create internal triggers —
emotions or contexts that automatically cue behaviour without any
external prompt.

**How this maps onto Attune:**

The internal trigger Attune should aim to create is not
"I want to check my notifications" but "I want to understand
what is happening between us." That shift — from external
notification-checking to internal curiosity — is the sign the
product has become genuinely habitual rather than just frequently opened.

The investment stage is Attune's most powerful mechanic. The data
a couple builds in Attune is genuinely irreplaceable — pattern memory,
pulse history, personal insights, game results. The longer they use
Attune, the more specific and accurate the intelligence becomes.
That investment creates the most ethical form of retention: the product
becomes more valuable the longer you use it, not because leaving is
painful, but because staying produces something worth having.

### 4.4 Don Norman's Three Levels of Emotional Design

Great design works at three levels: **visceral** (the immediate
sensory first impression), **behavioral** (how well the design
helps you complete a task), and **reflective** (what the product
means to you, the story it tells about you, how you remember
the experience).

**How this maps onto Attune:**

Most apps are designed at visceral and behavioral levels only.
Products with soul reach the reflective level — they become part
of how you think about yourself.

Attune's reflective design moments are the anniversary report,
the relationship post-mortem in healing mode, the personal pattern
portrait, and the accumulated personal insights. These are not
functional. They are the product saying: this chapter of your life
meant something, and here is how we witnessed it.

The product's primary colour — Attune green — is doing visceral design
work constantly and invisibly. See Section 4.6 for the colour philosophy.
The copy voice is doing reflective design work in every word it writes.

### 4.5 The Peak-End Rule — Daniel Kahneman

People do not judge experiences by their average. They judge by two
moments: the most intense point (the peak) and how the experience ends.

**How this maps onto Attune:**

Every primary user journey in Attune has a designed peak and a designed
ending. Nothing is allowed to simply fall off.

Kahneman also distinguishes between the **experiencing self** — the self
that lives in the present moment, moment by moment — and the
**remembering self** — the self that stores and narrates the past.
The remembering self does not record accurately. It edits toward
its existing beliefs, highlights peak moments, and rewrites history
around emotional intensity.

This distinction is the philosophical justification for Attune's
timeline logging. The timeline is an intervention on behalf of the
experiencing self. It creates a record of what actually happened —
the quiet good weeks, the repaired conflicts, the small moments of
genuine connection — that counteracts the remembering self's tendency
to rewrite the relationship around its most painful peaks.

When a couple says "we always fight about this" the timeline may
show they resolved it cleanly four times in the last six months.
That correction is not trivial. It is the difference between a
couple who believes they are stuck and a couple who can see they
are growing.

### 4.6 The Colour Philosophy — Attune Green

Attune's primary colour is a mature, desaturated green.

Not the bright green of money, growth charts, or environmental branding.
A grounded, living green — the colour of something real that grows over
time, quietly and without announcement.

This colour was chosen over teal (which reads as clinical and therapeutic),
pink and red (which read as romantic and dating-app conventional),
and purple (which reads as intelligent but cool).

Green reads as: alive, growing, grounded, natural, honest.
These are precisely the qualities Attune should communicate before
a user reads a single word. A relationship is a living thing. It grows
or it does not. Attune's colour should feel like something that grows.

The specific tone matters: desaturated enough to feel mature rather than
playful, dark enough to carry authority, warm enough not to feel clinical.
Every UI implementation must stay within this tonal range. Bright greens,
neon greens, and yellow-greens are off-brand regardless of context.

## 5. THE SEVEN DESIGN PRINCIPLES

These are the operational principles that follow from the
psychological framework. Every feature, every screen, every
notification, every line of copy should be evaluable against
at least one of them.

### Principle 1: Measure what is real, reward what is true

Every recognition moment in Attune is grounded in a measurable
change in real behaviour. Not badges for opening the app.
Not congratulations for completing a quiz. Competence measurement,
not badge theater.

"Your passive-aggressive phrasing dropped from 18% to 9% of conflict
messages" is a reward. "You completed the communication quiz" is not.
The number is the reward. The specificity is the reward.

No badges. No streaks. No leaderboards.
Only accurate mirrors that show people where they actually are,
where they were, and where they could go.

### Principle 2: Earn loss aversion through genuine value

Loss aversion is real — humans feel losses twice as intensely
as equivalent gains. Attune earns this through genuine value
accumulation, never through artificial lock-in mechanics.

The data a couple builds is genuinely irreplaceable. That is true.
Stating it clearly is honest product communication, not manipulation.
The fear of losing it is legitimate because the loss is real.

What Attune will never do: manufacture artificial lock-in by
making the free experience deliberately worse, by hiding data
in formats that cannot be used elsewhere, or by creating exit
friction designed to prevent cancellation rather than to surface
genuine value.

The data belongs to users. It is exportable. It is deletable.
The intelligence cannot be transferred because it is not a file —
it is understanding built from a specific relationship's specific
history. That understanding is inherently contextual. It would
be meaningless extracted from the relationship that generated it,
like exporting the feeling of understanding without the years of
experience that produced it. Users who leave Attune take their data,
their personal insights, and their growth with them. The only thing
that cannot travel is the system's accumulated understanding of
their specific relationship — not because we designed it that way,
but because that is the nature of understanding itself.

### Principle 3: Build infinite games around genuine infinity

Relationships genuinely have no done state. They are always
evolving, always presenting new dynamics, always requiring new
attention. The product is designed around a process that has
no endpoint because the relationship itself has no endpoint.

This is not a retention trick. It is an honest reflection of
what relationships are.

What Attune will never do: periodic resets that destroy genuine
progress to manufacture re-engagement. No artificial destruction
of data to create artificial motivation to rebuild.

### Principle 4: Design incomplete loops that close in the relationship

The best Zeigarnik loops in Attune are not in-app tasks.
They are insights that pull users toward conversations with
their partners.

"You have avoided this topic seven times — the loop closes
when you name it directly."

The app that creates pull toward real human connection,
rather than toward more app usage, is the product with genuine soul.

Every incomplete loop in Attune should be designed with this
question: when this loop closes, does it close inside the app
or inside the relationship? Prefer the relationship.

### Principle 5: Respect the user's autonomy absolutely

Attune never tells people what to decide. It surfaces patterns,
sources every claim, and steps back.

The reflective level — what the patterns mean for their life —
belongs entirely to the user. Attune's job ends at
"here is what the data shows." The user's job begins at
"here is what I choose to do about it."

This principle applies to every feature: the verdict,
the insights, the translator, the compatibility preview,
the post-mortem, the dating match. All of them are
information. None of them are instructions.

### Principle 6: Design for the hard moment, not the easy one

Every feature must work when motivation is low and ability
is depleted. After a conflict. During a difficult week.
When someone is flooded, exhausted, or hurting.

This is the Fogg principle made concrete. The conflict
translator needs to be two taps at the moment someone is
about to send something they will regret. The weekly
check-in needs to be thirty seconds on a Sunday when
someone is tired. The safety resources need to be one tap
from anywhere in the app.

If a feature only works when people feel good, it does not
work when it matters most.

### Principle 7: Be the anti-product in this category

In a category where most apps use dark patterns borrowed
from gaming and social media, Attune is visibly on the
other side.

Where others obscure, Attune clarifies.
Where others prevent decisions, Attune enables them.
Where others manufacture friction to sell relief,
Attune removes friction to deliver genuine value.
Where others design for maximum time-in-app,
Attune designs for maximum value-from-app.

This commitment is not just ethics. It is strategy.
A product competing on trust in an emotionally sensitive category
wins by being more transparent than competitors, not less.

## 6. THE GIFT NOT THE RECEIPT

Most apps deliver rewards as a receipt — flat, functional,
data-delivered. The best apps deliver rewards as a gift —
an experience with ceremony, anticipation, and afterglow.

Dopamine fires on anticipation, not on the reward itself.
The ceremony is not decoration. It is neurology.

### The three-stage gift sequence

**Stage 1: Anticipation**
Build uncertainty before the reveal to prime the brain.
The wait before the compatibility preview is not loading time.
It is anticipation time. Do not optimise it away.

**Stage 2: The reveal**
The moment needs visual weight, sequential unfolding, and
appropriate pace. The verdict headline appears alone first.
The game comparison reveals one person's answer at a time.
Nothing dumps all at once.

**Stage 3: The afterglow**
This is where long-term retention lives. The user sits
with the result. It becomes identity. The framing note
after a translated message. The thematic observation
after completing 36 questions. The closing agency prompt
after the monthly verdict.

### Where the gift sequence applies in Attune

```
Compatibility preview   → full gift sequence, highest ceremony
Monthly verdict         → full gift sequence, most weight
Conflict translator     → gift sequence, private afterglow
Game session reveal     → gift sequence per comparison moment
Level completion        → gift sequence, forward motion
Weekly check-in         → micro gift (AI observation), micro afterglow
Pulse score update      → gift delivery, trend as peak
Healing post-mortem     → gift sequence, closes on agency and meaning
Anniversary report      → Spotify Wrapped equivalent, highest shareability
```

### Where the gift sequence does NOT apply

Safety notifications must be delivered as clear, direct receipts.
No ceremony. No anticipation. No reveal sequence.
A person who may be at risk does not need dopamine-optimised design.
They need immediate, unambiguous access to help.

This is the permanent exception to the gift framework.
Safety is never a gift.

## 7. THE INTELLIGENCE TRAP — EARNED NOT MANUFACTURED

The intelligence trap is Attune's deepest moat and its most
important ethical commitment. Understanding the difference between
earned intelligence and manufactured lock-in is essential.

### What earned intelligence looks like

A couple can export their Attune data as structured files.
What they cannot transfer is the trained understanding of their
specific relationship — the pattern memory that knows this couple's
recurring conflict topics, the pulse model calibrated to their
baseline, the compatibility intelligence built from months of real
behaviour across chat, games, quizzes, and check-ins.

That intelligence is not a file. It is understanding built from
a specific relationship's specific history. It is inherently
contextual — it would be meaningless outside the relationship
that generated it, the way fluency in a language cannot be
transferred to someone who has not lived it.

This is not a design decision made to trap users.
It is the nature of understanding itself.

Users who leave Attune take everything that belongs to them:
their data, their personal insights, their growth metrics,
their quiz results, their timeline entries. The only thing
that does not travel is the system's accumulated comprehension
of their specific relationship — and that comprehension exists
to serve them, not to hold them.

### The three types of intelligence Attune builds

**Communication intelligence** — a model of how this specific person
communicates, what their patterns are under stress, what their baseline
tone looks like when the relationship is healthy. Built automatically
from chat analysis. Grows more accurate every month.

**Relationship intelligence** — the longitudinal picture of this specific
relationship's health. Pulse calibrated to their baseline, not to a
generic benchmark. The meaning of a 74 pulse score for this couple
is different from what it means for any other couple.

**Pattern intelligence** — specific knowledge about this relationship's
dynamics, built session by session over months. Not generic relationship
advice. Knowledge about this couple's recurring topics, repair patterns,
and growth trajectory.

### Making intelligence visible

Users should always be able to see the investment they have built.
Not as a lock-in tactic. As honest communication of genuine value.

"847 messages read · 18 patterns tracked · 24 weeks of data"
is not a retention mechanic. It is a true statement about what exists.

### Automating the deposit

Attune's intelligence builds automatically. Users train the AI
without consciously trying to. Every message, every conflict,
every game played, every check-in completed — all of these
deposit intelligence as a byproduct of living in the relationship.

The only thing users need to do is be in their relationship.
The product does the rest.

## 8. WHAT ATTUNE WILL NEVER DO

These are permanent constraints. No feature request, no business
pressure, no growth target changes any of these. Ever.

### Psychological manipulation

```
NEVER: Streak mechanics designed to create obligation rather than motivation
NEVER: Artificially degraded free experience to sell paid relief
NEVER: Variable coin pricing designed to prevent mental math of real cost
NEVER: Manufactured random rewards to create slot-machine compulsion
NEVER: Exit friction designed to prevent cancellation
NEVER: Periodic data resets to manufacture re-engagement motivation
NEVER: Notifications designed around DAU optimisation rather than user need
NEVER: Gamification of relationship health that turns intimacy into a score
```

### Privacy violations

```
NEVER: Asymmetric behavioural data shown about a partner to the other partner
NEVER: A couple report combining both users' personal insights
NEVER: Safety event data shared with the non-at-risk partner
NEVER: Cycle data shared without explicit opt-in
NEVER: Forum anonymous identity linked to real profile in any public surface
NEVER: Raw message content in AI prompt logs
NEVER: Relationship data used for advertising targeting
```

### Clinical overreach

```
NEVER: Diagnostic language — toxic, narcissist, codependent, disorder, broken
NEVER: Telling users what to decide about their relationship
NEVER: Attributing negative behaviour to a named or implied partner
NEVER: Generating content that could be used as evidence against a partner
NEVER: Positioning the AI as a substitute for professional clinical help
NEVER: Safety features positioned as guarantees of safety
```

### Trust violations

```
NEVER: Pricing denominated in virtual currency designed to obscure real cost
NEVER: Hidden fees or charges not clearly stated before purchase
NEVER: Data sold to third parties
NEVER: Patterns or insights used for purposes other than helping that user
NEVER: The recipient ever knowing a message was rewritten by the translator
NEVER: Manipulating the intelligence visible to a user based on retention goals
NEVER: Using past relationship data for dating compatibility without
        explicit separate consent from the user
```

## 9. THE ETHICAL BOUNDARIES BY FEATURE

For each major feature, the ethical line between what Attune
does and what it must never do.

### Chat analysis

**Does:** Read messages to detect patterns, tone, and communication
signals that help users understand their own behaviour over time.

**Never does:** Share individual message analysis with a partner.
Never creates a per-message report visible to anyone other than
the sender about their own messages.

**The line:** Session-level patterns are shared. Message-level
attribution is private to the sender.

### Pulse score

**Does:** Compute a relationship health picture from aggregated
behavioural data across multiple sources, shown to both partners.

**Never does:** Benchmark a couple against other named couples.
Never uses the score to create anxiety by design. Never adjusts
the score to create engagement rather than to reflect reality.

**The line:** The score reflects what the data shows.
It is never adjusted for retention purposes.

### Conflict translator

**Does:** Offer an alternative phrasing of a message when the user
explicitly requests it. Private to the sender. Recipient never knows.

**Never does:** Appear automatically. Interrupt a user mid-message.
Suggest the user is communicating wrongly when they did not ask.

**The line:** Pull not push. Opt-in per message, always.

### Safety system

**Does:** Detect explicit language patterns that may signal risk
and deliver quiet, private resources to the at-risk user.

**Never does:** Alert the potentially dangerous partner.
Never diagnoses abuse. Never positions itself as a safety guarantee.
Never uses an AI model to make safety determinations — hard-coded
keyword detection only.

**The line:** The safety system protects quietly. It never accuses.
It never promises protection it cannot guarantee.

### Dating mode — data consent

**Does:** Generate a compatibility profile from the user's own
behavioural data — attachment patterns, communication style,
love language, conflict defaults — accumulated during their
time on Attune.

**Requires explicit separate consent for:**
Using data from a previous relationship stored in Attune
to inform dating compatibility. This is not assumed. It is
not bundled into general terms of service. It is a specific,
clear, standalone opt-in presented at the point of entering
the dating pool: "Your past relationship data can improve
your matches. Use it?" The user chooses yes or no.

**Never does:** Use another person's data — a previous partner —
in the dating algorithm without that person's awareness. The data
used for dating compatibility is always the current user's own
observed behaviour, never their ex-partner's profile or patterns.

**The line:** Your intelligence from your relationship is yours
to use in dating if you choose. Your ex's patterns are theirs.
These are never combined into a shared compatibility signal.

### Forum

**Does:** Provide anonymous text discussion of relationship topics
with relationship status context but no identity.

**Never does:** Allow ranking of posts by relationship health metrics.
Never creates visible status hierarchies based on relationship quality.
Never links anonymous forum identity to real profile in any public surface.

**The line:** Community belonging without competition.
The forum is connection, not comparison.

### Gamification

**Does:** Apply completion drive (visible arcs), variable genuine
discovery (verdicts, translator results), and hyper-local comparison
(pulse score benchmarks against similar couples).

**Never does:** Badges for using features. Streaks designed to
create obligation. Leaderboards of any kind. Manufactured randomness.

**The line:** Gamification mechanics are permitted only when
the variability or achievement is genuine — grounded in real
intelligence or real growth, never in artificial surprise
or manufactured competition.

## 10. THE VOICE OF ATTUNE

Attune has one consistent voice across every word it generates —
in notifications, in insights, in verdicts, in onboarding,
in error states, in empty states, in every line of copy.

This voice is grounded in Carl Rogers' three conditions for
therapeutic presence: unconditional positive regard, empathy,
and congruence. These are not therapy concepts imported into
a product — they are the precise description of how a wise,
honest friend speaks.

### The voice is

**Honest.** It says what the data shows, nothing more.
It does not soften difficult observations into meaninglessness.
It does not amplify mild patterns into alarming ones.
It says the true thing in the clearest possible way.
This is Rogers' congruence — authenticity without performance.

**Specific.** Generic observations have no value.
"You communicate well" is worthless. "Your assertive phrasing
increased from 40% to 68% of conflict responses over four months"
is worth something. Specificity is the proof that the product
is paying attention.

**Warm.** Not clinical. Not cheerful. Not preachy.
The voice of a wise, attentive friend who has been paying
close attention and shares observations with care.
The friend who tells you true things you need to hear
in a way that makes you feel seen rather than judged.
This is Rogers' unconditional positive regard — acceptance
without conditions, presence without agenda.

**Respectful.** The user is always capable of making their
own decisions. The voice never condescends, never pressures,
never assumes it knows better than the person living the relationship.
This is Rogers' empathy — understanding the user's frame of
reference without imposing a different one.

**Grounded.** Every claim is sourced. "Based on your chat
analysis" not "it seems like." "Your last 8 sessions" not
"recently." The grounding is what separates intelligence
from opinion.

### The voice is never

Therapeutic jargon that pathologises normal behaviour.
Cheerful positivity that papers over difficult observations.
Alarming language that creates anxiety to drive engagement.
Vague observations that could apply to any relationship.
Instructions dressed up as insights.

### The verdict voice specifically

Because the word "verdict" carries the risk of sounding
like a judgment, every verdict must open with a specific,
data-grounded observation — never a general assessment.

Wrong: "Your relationship is showing signs of strain."
Right: "Connection has declined 11 points over three months.
Here is where that shows in the data."

Wrong: "You are communicating well."
Right: "Passive-aggressive phrasing dropped from 18% to 9%
of conflict messages since January."

The verdict is the product at its most visible. It must be
the product at its most precise.

### Sample voice test

Ask: does this sound like the same person who wrote every
other line in the product? Does it sound honest, specific,
warm, respectful, and grounded? Is it something a wise
friend would say, or something a checklist would produce?

If it reads like a checklist, rewrite it.

## 11. THE ANTI-PRODUCTS — WHAT WE ARE NOT BUILDING

Understanding what Attune is not requires understanding two specific
products that represent the design philosophies Attune explicitly rejects.

### ReelShort — the monetisation anti-product

ReelShort's three core patterns represent everything Attune
refuses to do with monetisation:

The graduated gate with manufactured friction — making the free
experience deliberately worse to sell paid relief. Attune's free
tier is genuinely valuable, not artificially limited.

The anti-calculator — variable pricing and awkward coin ratios
designed to prevent users from understanding what they spend.
Attune's pricing is always in real currency, always clearly stated,
always cancellable without friction.

The zero-decision player — eliminating stopping points and progress
indicators to prevent conscious decisions to stop using the app.
Attune's exit flow is as frictionless as its entry.

ReelShort's underlying philosophy: users cannot be trusted to make
decisions good for the business, so the product should prevent them
from making decisions at all.

Attune's philosophy: users should always be able to make fully
informed decisions about what they pay, how long they use the app,
and whether they stay.

### Tinder — the engagement anti-product

Tinder is the most successful relationship app in history by every
engagement metric. It produces the worst relationship outcomes
by every psychological measure. Users are maximally engaged and
minimally served. The slot machine replaced the relationship.
The match replaced the date.

Attune's dating mode must never reproduce any of these mechanics:
swipe interfaces that create dopamine from uncertainty, match counts
that make accumulation feel like achievement, confetti animations
that turn a human connection into a slot machine win, or session
design optimised for maximum time-in-app rather than maximum
movement toward real connection.

Tinder's design philosophy: make the pursuit of connection
more satisfying than connection itself.

Attune's design philosophy: make the pursuit of connection
lead to connection itself, as quickly as possible.

The match is the beginning of something real, not the product.

## 12. THE SOUL IN ONE SENTENCE

> Attune is designed to be worth returning to —
> not designed to make leaving feel painful.

Everything else in this document is an elaboration of that sentence.

The intelligence compounds because real intelligence compounds,
not because we engineered a trap. The incomplete loops pull users
back because the relationship is genuinely unfinished, not because
we manufactured artificial incompleteness. The gift ceremonies feel
meaningful because the intelligence delivered is genuinely meaningful,
not because we optimised a dopamine sequence.

The product earns its place in people's lives by genuinely
understanding them better over time.

That is what it means to have soul.

## 14. THE PSYCHOLOGICAL HERITAGE

This section documents the eight thinkers whose work shaped
Attune's design philosophy. Each one has a specific contribution.
Together they form the intellectual architecture of the product.

For the clinical validation document covering instruments and
measurement methodology, see ATTUNE_CLINICAL.md (forthcoming).

### John Bowlby (1907–1990) — The Foundation

Bowlby founded attachment theory. His central insight was the
internal working model: the mental representation of what a
relationship is, formed from early caregiver interactions and
carried into every adult relationship.

The internal working model is the most important concept for
understanding why Attune's attachment quiz predicts real behaviour.
When the AI detects a pursue-withdraw pattern in a user's chat,
it is detecting their internal working model playing out in real
time — the mental template formed in childhood, running
automatically in adulthood.

Bowlby's contribution to Attune: the theoretical foundation that
explains why attachment patterns measured in a quiz actually
predict communication behaviour in a relationship years later.
Without Bowlby the quiz is a personality test. With Bowlby it is
a window into the deepest relational architecture a person carries.

His work: A Secure Base (1988). Attachment and Loss trilogy
(1969, 1973, 1980).

### Carl Jung (1875–1961) — The Mirror

Jung founded analytical psychology and gave us four concepts
that are directly operational in Attune's design.

**The Shadow** — the unconscious parts of ourselves that we have
rejected or never developed. The shadow does not disappear when
repressed; it leaks into relationships as projection, self-sabotage,
and the conviction that the problem is always the other person.
Attune's pattern memory is, in Jungian terms, a technology for
bringing the shadow into partial consciousness. The user who sees
their pursue-withdraw pattern quantified is seeing a shadow
behaviour made visible for the first time.

**Projection** — the mechanism by which we attribute our own
unconscious qualities to another person. Jung observed that
romantic attraction is heavily projection-driven: we fall for
people who carry qualities we have not yet integrated in ourselves.
When the projection fades — as it always does — what remains is
either a real relationship or nothing.
For Attune's dating mode: intelligence-first matching helps users
see past the projection to a more honest compatibility. The app
is not replacing chemistry — it is adding the dimension that
chemistry alone cannot provide.

**Individuation** — the lifelong process of becoming fully and
authentically yourself by integrating all parts of the psyche,
including the shadow. Jung believed this was the deepest human
developmental task.
For Attune's healing mode: the post-mortem report is not just
"here is what went wrong." In Jungian terms it is the beginning
of individuation — understanding which parts of yourself the
relationship was reflecting back to you, which parts you projected
onto your partner, and which parts you still need to integrate.
Healing mode is where individuation begins.

**The Persona** — the social mask we wear, the identity we
perform for the world. There is always some element of pretence
in the persona; it is the shop window, not the warehouse.
The gap between what users report about their communication style
(their persona) and what the AI observes in their actual messages
(the real pattern) is one of Attune's most valuable insights.
Jung would recognise this immediately as the gap between
persona and shadow.

His work: The Collected Works, especially Volumes 7 and 17.
Aion (1951). Marriage as a Psychological Relationship (1925).

### Sue Johnson (1947–2024) — The Science of Love

Sue Johnson created Emotionally Focused Therapy — the most
empirically validated couples therapy approach in existence,
with 35+ years of peer-reviewed research and 70-75% efficacy
rates in randomised controlled trials.

Her central insight, built directly on Bowlby's work: romantic
love is fundamentally an attachment bond. Every relationship
fight — about dishes, money, time, attention — is ultimately
a fight about attachment: Are you there for me? Do I matter to
you? Can I count on you? The content of the argument is almost
never the real issue. The attachment threat underneath it always is.

This reframes everything about Attune's conflict analysis.
The root cause detection — identifying whether the underlying
need in a conflict is respect, fairness, affection, security,
autonomy, or rest — is Johnsonian. The pursue-withdraw pattern
is the central dynamic of her research: the anxious partner
pursues because separation triggers the attachment system;
the avoidant partner withdraws because closeness threatens their
defensive independence. Both are expressions of the same
underlying attachment fear.

Her contribution to Attune's voice: the insight that people in
relationship distress do not need correction — they need to feel
felt. The product should never communicate in a way that makes
users feel managed. It should communicate in a way that makes
them feel understood.

Her work: Hold Me Tight (2008). Love Sense (2013). The Practice
of Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy (2020).

### Marshall Rosenberg (1934–2015) — The Language

Rosenberg developed Nonviolent Communication — a four-component
framework for expressing observations, feelings, needs, and
requests without judgment or blame.

His core insight: beneath every accusation is an unmet need.
"You never listen to me" is an accusation. The need beneath it
is to feel heard. When people learn to express the need rather
than the accusation, the other person's defensiveness drops
and genuine dialogue becomes possible.

The NVC four-component structure — observation, feeling, need,
request — is the implicit algorithm of Attune's conflict
translator. Every rewrite moves the message from accusation
toward need expression. The framing note that appears after
the rewrite — "underlying need: to feel heard" — is Rosenberg's
contribution made explicit and specific.

Important caveat: NVC has weaker peer-reviewed validation than
attachment theory or Gottman's research. It is practitioner
wisdom of the highest quality, applied in 60 countries, but
it does not have the same randomised controlled trial base.
FRAMEWORK_CONFIDENCE for NVC detection: medium.

His work: Nonviolent Communication: A Language of Life (2003).

### Abraham Maslow (1908–1970) — The Needs Map

Maslow's hierarchy of needs provides the vocabulary for the
root needs layer in Attune's conflict analysis. Love and
belonging needs — the third tier of his hierarchy — are the
direct source of the six root need categories the AI uses
when identifying what a conflict is really about.

His contribution is not as a design framework (SDT supersedes
him for that purpose) but as a taxonomist of human needs. The
categories of security, affection, respect, fairness, autonomy,
and rest that appear in Attune's session analysis are a refined
subset of Maslow's hierarchy applied to the specific domain of
intimate relationship conflict.

Maslow also connects to the soul document's core belief:
self-actualisation — the highest level of his hierarchy —
requires first meeting the love and belonging needs beneath it.
A person who does not feel genuinely connected and known by
their partner cannot reach their fullest self. Attune exists
at the love and belonging tier. It is, in Maslovian terms,
an infrastructure for the most important human need above
physical survival.

His work: A Theory of Human Motivation (1943).
Motivation and Personality (1954).

### Carl Rogers (1902–1987) — The Voice

Rogers founded client-centred therapy and identified the three
conditions that enable genuine human growth: unconditional
positive regard, empathy, and congruence.

His central conviction: people do not change because they are
told what to do or shown what is wrong with them. They change
when they are genuinely heard and accepted without conditions —
when they feel safe enough to see themselves clearly.

This is the exact philosophical foundation of Attune's voice
and of its central design constraint that the product never
tells users what to decide. Attune does not direct. It reflects.
It creates the conditions in which people can see themselves
and decide for themselves. That is client-centred design.

The three Rogerian conditions translated into product voice:
Unconditional positive regard becomes warmth — acceptance
without judgment, no matter what the data shows.
Empathy becomes specificity — meeting the user in their actual
situation, not a generic version of it.
Congruence becomes honesty — saying what the data shows,
even when it is uncomfortable, without softening it into
meaninglessness.

Rogers' contribution to Attune is invisible in the features
and visible in every sentence the product writes.

His work: On Becoming a Person (1961).
Client-Centred Therapy (1951).

### Viktor Frankl (1905–1997) — The Meaning

Frankl was an Austrian psychiatrist and Holocaust survivor who
founded logotherapy — therapy built on the premise that the
primary human motivation is not pleasure or power but meaning.
His book Man's Search for Meaning is one of the most-read
psychology books in history.

His contribution to Attune is specifically in the healing mode
and in the personal pattern portrait.

When a relationship ends, the question that most needs answering
is not "what went wrong" but "what did this mean, and what do
I carry forward." Recovery is not the goal of Attune's healing
mode — meaning is. The post-mortem report, in Frankl's terms,
is meaning-making: understanding what this relationship revealed
about you, what you learned, what you want to bring into the
next chapter. The closing prompt of the healing mode post-mortem
— "what is one thing you want to do differently?" — is a
Frankl-ian act. It turns pain into intention.

The personal pattern portrait in Tracks B and C is also Frankl.
"Here is how you show up in relationships" is not just profiling —
it is identity-level meaning-making. The user is being invited
to understand themselves not as a fixed type but as a person
with a specific history of choices and patterns and the capacity
to choose differently.

Frankl's insight that meaning can be found even in suffering
is the deepest philosophical foundation of the healing mode.
The relationship that ended was not wasted. It taught something.
Attune's job is to help users find what that something is.

His work: Man's Search for Meaning (1946).

### Brené Brown (b. 1965) — The Permission

Brown is a research professor at the University of Houston who
has spent two decades studying shame, vulnerability, courage,
and empathy using grounded theory methodology.

Her central finding: vulnerability is not weakness. It is the
birthplace of connection. Shame — the fear of being unworthy
of connection — is the barrier between people and genuine
intimacy. The people with the strongest sense of belonging are
those who believe they are worthy of love and belonging without
conditions. This is almost exactly the definition of secure
attachment, which makes Brown's work a bridge between attachment
theory and the lived experience of what connection feels like
from the inside.

Her contribution to Attune is in two specific places.

The 36 Questions game works because of what Brown's research
explains. The questions create closeness not as a side effect
of the intimacy but as the direct result of mutual vulnerability.
Being genuinely seen creates connection. Being genuinely seen
requires courage. The game is a structured permission to be
seen — by a partner, by yourself.

The anonymous forum is also Brown's design. Anonymous text
discussion allows vulnerability without the risk of identity
exposure. The design decision to keep the forum text-only and
anonymous is not just a privacy choice — it is a vulnerability-
enabling choice. People will say what they actually think when
they are not afraid of being judged for it. The forum is a
space where shame cannot operate because identity cannot be known.

Her work: Daring Greatly (2012).
The Gifts of Imperfection (2010).
Brené Brown's TED Talk: The Power of Vulnerability (2010) —
one of the five most-watched TED Talks in history.
